# Open Hardware Control 3.4.23.2 INTERN

3.4.23.2 focuses on the Cooling user experience and exclusive ownership of motherboard fan control.

- Compact CPU/Kraken and chassis-fan dashboard.
- CPU_FAN/PUMP_FAN stay out of chassis PWM control.
- Full-width chassis-fan cards with RPM/PWM, preset, sensor and curve preview.
- CoolerControl conflict detection and explicit systemd/Polkit takeover/release.
- Guided 30%/80% chassis-fan contrast test with ten-second RPM observation and full state restoration.
- Optional white RGB identification plus persistent PWM-to-RGB/case-layout mapping.
